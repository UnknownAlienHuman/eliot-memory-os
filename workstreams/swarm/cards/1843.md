Mark authority, lease, route, audit, external-effect, verifier, finish, recovery, and control-loss boundaries as full evidence. If optional diagnostic detail is sampled or disabled, emit a bounded coverage/blind-interval indicator and preserve the denominator. Add a qualification expiry and removal/kill condition so telemetry collection is periodically re-justified."
