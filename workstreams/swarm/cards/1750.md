Second START, the causal order in `bins/eliot-host/src/lib.rs`. Today the readiness contour runs:

1. `reobserve_watchdog_supervision_evidence` → `host_startup_evidence::build_host_startup_evidence`
2. `jobs.probe_kernel_readiness(..., &supervision_evidence)` → Kernel `record_live_supervision_evidence` marks step 11

So a `Running` Watchdog whose heartbeat responder is wedged still produces a fresh, current, authority-bearing step-11 observation with an advancing `supervision_progress_frontier`.

Third START, revocation exists but is fired on the wrong trigger. `git grep revoke_supervision_evidence origin/main -- bins crates` returns exactly two call sites, and the only one — `bins/eliot-kernel/src/control_plane.rs` — is gated on `KernelControlCommand::Activate(_) | KernelControlCommand::ReconcileActivation(_)`. There is **no** revocation on a Host heartbeat-admission failure, so `StartupCoordinator::admit_supervision_observation` keeps authorising Material/Critical work for the rest of the observation's validity interval.

**MAKE:** Bind step 11 to the admitted heartbeat, and revoke on its failure.

(a) **Carry the heartbeat proof to Kernel.** `HostObservedWatchdogHeartbeat` already holds every field the binding needs: `watchdog_readiness_sequence`, `handshake_count`, `host_boot_id`, `host_receive_monotonic_ms`, `host_receive_wall_ms`, `host_receive_monotonic` (an `Instant`, in-process only), `service_instance_guid`, `pipe_name`, `watchdog_incarnation_pid`, `watchdog_incarnation_start_100ns`, `kernel_epoch`, `watchdog_epoch`, `tick_interval_ms`, and its `coverage`. Add a typed, `deny_unknown_fields` heartbeat-proof field to the supervision-evidence carrier in `crates/kernel/eliot-kernel-service/src/protocol.rs` — either on `HostStartupEvidence` or, if the two-stage protocol is simpler, on `HostStartupEvidenceReport` — and validate its shape in the same `validate` that `bins/eliot-kernel/src/control_plane.rs` already calls for `ReportHostStartupEvidence`. Bind the consumer `StateFence` alongside it.

(b) **Stop counting the same PID as progress.** In `bins/eliot-kernel/src/startup_coordinator.rs`, derive `supervision_progress_frontier` from the owner-observed Watchdog progress — the admitted `watchdog_readiness_sequence` (or the receipt identity) — not from `saturating_add(1)`. An exact replay of the same heartbeat must preserve the same observation without creating new progress and without refreshing responsiveness.

(c) **Reorder the Host readiness contour.** In `bins/eliot-host/src/lib.rs`, verify the SCM incarnation, obtain the fresh admitted heartbeat, then report the combined evidence to Kernel, and only then let `ProbeReady` and Material/Critical admission proceed. A two-stage protocol is acceptable **only** if stage one cannot mark step 11 and cannot authorise supervised work.

(d) **Revoke on heartbeat-admission failure.** In `bins/eliot-kernel/src/control_plane.rs` (and the Kernel-side composition seam in `bins/eliot-kernel/src/lib.rs`), atomically revoke or contradict the matching supervision observation when the Host reports a heartbeat failure, before the degraded Host result is returned. A revocation failure must leave Material/Critical admission fenced or unknown — never current. Also make `KernelComposition::admit_material_authority_for_fence` consume the heartbeat-bound observation, or a Kernel-owned digest derived from it; Host-local heartbeat refs alone cannot close a Kernel admission edge.
