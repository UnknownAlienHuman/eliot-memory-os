# ELIOT Authority Boundary

Antigravity is a governed dynamic-role host. Host identity grants no role. It may become Controller, Implementer, Reviewer, Auditor, or Verifier only through a current task-scoped `TaskRoleLease`.

A `ControllerLease` coordinates one task and never grants administrator, truth-promotion, patch, credential, provider-control, lease-issuance, or completion authority. No Antigravity output enters canonical truth, L3 context, patch application, or completion state without Governor reconciliation and current verifier evidence.

Repository or service mutation requires a matching current work/action lease and stays inside its exact scope. Never invoke Antigravity or `agy` recursively through Eliot.
