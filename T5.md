## T5 — Native worker: claim-bound execution and provider adapters behind it (#22 + #874)

### 1. Decision

The execution boundary remains the existing A-13 `WorkerCore`/`NativeWorker` contour, with P-03 `eliot_process::ProcessExecutor` as the only governed process lifecycle contract; neither the registry nor a provider adapter becomes another process, admission, recovery, or task owner (`crates/modules/eliot-native-worker-core/src/lib.rs:1@b71d74c0`; `docs/architecture/I10-08-02-ip0-one-windows-processexecutor.md:3@b71d74c0`).
Governor supplies semantic admission, the registered attempt and launch outbox, while Kernel stages and activates the matching reservation and issues/enforces launch authority only after the canonical receipt and current fence agree; the worker does not manufacture a permit from a claim, heartbeat, catalogue row or descriptor (`docs/architecture/I10-15-agent-execution-fabric-and-durable-swarm.md:258@b71d74c0`; `docs/architecture/I10-15-agent-execution-fabric-and-durable-swarm.md:261@b71d74c0`; `docs/architecture/I06-15-capability-grant-lineage-introductions-and-resource-facets.md:3@b71d74c0`).
The claim consumer must join the authenticated registration, exact claim, activated authority, admitted route/facets and process binding before execution; `NativeWorkerClaim` alone is deliberately insufficient to construct `WorkerHello` or an authority-bearing `ProcessRequest` (`docs/architecture/A12-02-principal-session-and-visibility.md:3@b71d74c0`; `crates/modules/eliot-native-worker-core/src/protocol.rs:85@b71d74c0`; `crates/modules/eliot-native-worker-core/src/protocol.rs:745@b71d74c0`).
Place the finite provider-factory composition in `bins/eliot-native-worker/src/adapter_registry.rs`, under #874, consuming the four existing adapter packages rather than importing provider execution into the coordinator; factory lookup realizes an already admitted exact route, not a second route-selection policy (`crates/agent/eliot-agent-claude/AGENTS.md:7@b71d74c0`; `docs/architecture/I06-15-capability-grant-lineage-introductions-and-resource-facets.md:3@b71d74c0`; `docs/architecture/I10-11-external-model-bridges.md:3@b71d74c0`).
Replace `GapProvider` only through a concrete, closed verifier implementation inside the coordinator's existing sealed seam, backed by authenticated current owner evidence; retain the explicit plan-only constructor and do not expose `ProviderVerifier` for arbitrary callers to implement (`crates/agent/eliot-agent-coordinator/src/core.rs:39@b71d74c0`; `docs/architecture/A12-02-principal-session-and-visibility.md:3@b71d74c0`).
For user-bound routes, consume the exact User Broker registration and resource/credential leases, without transferring the broker's signing material or a user's secrets into the service or worker environment (`docs/architecture/I01-03-optional-and-on-demand-processes.md:23@b71d74c0`; `docs/architecture/I15-04-secrets.md:3@b71d74c0`; `docs/architecture/I06-15-capability-grant-lineage-introductions-and-resource-facets.md:3@b71d74c0`).
Claim reconciliation, worker event replay and process reconciliation remain distinct operations under their existing owners; uncertainty cannot be cleared by a new claim, provider switch or fresh worker generation (`bins/eliot-kernel/src/native_worker_reconcile_route.rs:3@b71d74c0`; `crates/modules/eliot-native-worker-core/src/ports.rs:905@b71d74c0`; `docs/architecture/I10-15-agent-execution-fabric-and-durable-swarm.md:244@b71d74c0`).
T2 must close the actual start/observation/IO boundary, and T4 must preserve the admitted attempt through every provider event and receipt, before #874 can claim executable factory coverage; current Claude contract code cannot be counted as a completed SDK execution path (`docs/architecture/I10-08-02-ip0-one-windows-processexecutor.md:3@b71d74c0`; `crates/agent/eliot-agent-api/src/lib.rs:10@b71d74c0`; `crates/agent/eliot-agent-codex/src/lib.rs:317@b71d74c0`; `crates/agent/eliot-agent-claude/src/lib.rs:13@b71d74c0`).

### 3. Current code reality

**The worker has useful mechanics, but its binary is not executing claims.** `bins/eliot-native-worker/src/lib.rs` wraps `WorkerCore` with asynchronous `start`, `recover`, `handle` and `serve_stdio`. The core accepts injected process, capability-admission, replay, checkpoint and evidence ports. `demand_start` validates the binding, obtains capability facts, starts through the executor, checks observed process evidence and publishes readiness. It is not an empty worker implementation. However, `bins/eliot-native-worker/src/main.rs:12@b71d74c0` still converts even a successful Kernel connection into `PROVIDER_RUNTIME_DEFERRED` and exits 78.

**The older “connect always refuses” audit must not be copied forward.** The pinned `kernel_admission_client.rs` already opens transport and implements registration, claim, readiness, heartbeat, checkpoint, result submission and cancellation observation. These methods retain checked local projections of successful responses. They do not supply the missing complete claim-to-core execution composition. JSON response shape, echoed identifiers and a `receipt_digest` are not, by themselves, the authenticated/current capability required by #1108. Preserve the transport work; replace incomplete authority joins, not the whole client.

**The Kernel side is further along than the task's original blocker list.** `frame_dispatch.rs` routes native lifecycle requests after service/session checks; `native_worker_lifecycle_route.rs` delegates stage/load/advance operations to the ORS claim table and claim/readiness decisions to Kernel. `native_worker_reconcile_route.rs` supplies **`native_worker.reconcile`**, an eighth operation in the Kernel dispatcher. Its exact-claim readback is not the core's stream/event journal. The worker client's seven lifecycle operations still need the corresponding reconciliation consumer (`bins/eliot-kernel/src/frame_dispatch.rs:140@b71d74c0`; `bins/eliot-kernel/src/native_worker_lifecycle_route.rs:82@b71d74c0`; `bins/eliot-kernel/src/native_worker_reconcile_route.rs:3@b71d74c0`).

**Reuse these boundaries instead of renaming them:**

- Claim/registration/readiness projections: `crates/modules/eliot-native-worker-core/src/protocol.rs`; `ClaimAdmissionRequest` and `ReadinessSubmission`: `src/ports.rs`.
- Kernel claim wire: `crates/kernel/eliot-kernel-service/src/protocol/native_worker_claim.rs`, including `NativeWorkerClaimRequest` and `NativeWorkerClaimResponse`. The Kernel and worker projections have a deliberately matching canonical binding digest; changing only one side is a protocol defect.
- Capability/effect checks: `CapabilityAdmissionPort`; durable checkpoint receipts: `DurableCheckpointPort`; event identity, replay and acknowledgements: `DurableReplayPort`. Preserve their separate responsibilities.
- Process front door: `KernelProcessExecutionClient`, `ProcessStarter`, `ProcessOperationPort`, `ProcessExecutionRequest`; P-03 start consumes the authority-bearing `ProcessRequest`. The service crate explicitly forbids importing the concrete executor; concrete implementations belong to the binary composition (`crates/kernel/eliot-kernel-service/src/process_execution_client.rs:8@b71d74c0`).
- Durable mechanics: `eliot_ors::{OperationalRecoveryStore, OrsCoordinator, RedbRecoveryStore}` and native claim records. `OperationIdentity` is an opaque operational label, not permission (`crates/kernel/eliot-ors/src/lib.rs:1@b71d74c0`; `crates/kernel/eliot-ors/src/model.rs:50@b71d74c0`).

**Provider packages are not interchangeable “ready” implementations.** Codex has `attach`, `CodexAdapter<E>::launch/cancel/reconcile_unknown`, an injected P-03 executor and one-shot `Option<ProcessRequest>`. Its launch/cancel receipts reconstruct `AttemptId` from `attached.launch.id`, whereas `begin_attempt` accepts an independent attempt ID: T4 must close that identity split before production composition. ACP supplies bounded framing, session/attempt translation and `AcpProcessBinding`; its header explicitly assigns physical execution to P-03. OpenCode exposes the HTTP/SSE `OpenCodeClient` and a read-only bootstrap policy, not proof of arbitrary Material execution. Reuse only the exact supported and admitted contour (`crates/agent/eliot-agent-codex/src/lib.rs:317@b71d74c0`; `crates/agent/eliot-agent-acp/src/lib.rs:1@b71d74c0`; `crates/agent/eliot-agent-opencode/src/lib.rs:3@b71d74c0`; `crates/agent/eliot-agent-opencode/src/client.rs:17@b71d74c0`).

**Claude remains a prerequisite, despite the landed changes.** Its source includes additional normalization/idempotency helpers and `ClaudeLaunchPort::check_admission`, but `admit_with_port` only validates/digests a plan and delegates admission; it does not launch the SDK sidecar. `AdmittedHandle` contains a request ID and plan digest and expressly carries no fence, epoch or authority. The package's runtime exclusion and #1112 ownership are explicit. Do not interpret the existence of those helpers, or PR #1246, as execution proof (`crates/agent/eliot-agent-claude/src/lib.rs:13@b71d74c0`; `crates/agent/eliot-agent-claude/src/lib.rs:1212@b71d74c0`; `crates/agent/eliot-agent-claude/AGENTS.md:25@b71d74c0`).

**The coordinator's gap is real.** `ProviderVerifier` is crate-private; `AgentCoordinator::new` installs `GapProvider`; `with_provider` is crate-private. The production constructor does not yet accept an authenticated provider capability. Catalogue observations under #265 and pure eligibility under #694 must not be converted to `ProviderBindingSnapshot::Verified` to bypass that gap (`crates/agent/eliot-agent-coordinator/src/core.rs:39@b71d74c0`; `docs/architecture/I06-15-capability-grant-lineage-introductions-and-resource-facets.md:3@b71d74c0`).
